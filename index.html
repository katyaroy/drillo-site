Language Drills

Micro-drills for spoken French, Italian and Dutch.

One-tap cards with spaced repetition: a verb engine covering every tense, grammar from A1 to C1, serialised stories, two-voice dialogues, words that always carry their article, phrases, numbers by ear, and a hands-free Commute mode you can do with the screen locked.

No streaks, no guilt, no manipulation. Motivation comes from content worth reading.

Common questions

Does Drillo need an internet connection?

No. Everything — the content, the drills, the speech — runs on your device. The app makes no network connections at all.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and nothing to log in to. See the privacy policy.

Why does the "Say it" drill ask for the microphone?

To hear the phrase you say and check it. It is optional — decline it and everything else still works. Audio is handed to your device's own speech recognition and never stored by the app.

How do I start a language over?

Settings → "Start over from zero". It clears the progress for the language you are currently studying.

Is the content checked by native speakers?

Not yet. The material is a carefully built draft and is still being reviewed. If you spot something that a native speaker would never say, please report it — those reports are the fastest way the content improves.
