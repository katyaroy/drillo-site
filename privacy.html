1. Introduction

1.1 This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how information is handled in connection with Drillo — Language Drills (the "App"), a mobile application for iOS and Android providing language-learning exercises in French, Italian and Dutch.

1.2 The App is developed and published by the independent developer of Drillo ("we", "us", "our"), who acts as the data controller in respect of the App for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "GDPR").

1.3 This Policy applies solely to the App. It does not apply to the application stores through which the App is distributed, to the operating system on your device, or to any third-party service to which you may choose to send information yourself.

1.4 By downloading, installing or using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy.

2. Summary

2.1 The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

2.2 The App contains no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking technologies.

2.3 The App functions entirely offline and initiates no network connections of its own.

2.4 All learning data is stored locally on your device and remains under your sole control.

2.5 This section is provided for convenience only and does not limit or replace the sections that follow.

3. Information we do not collect

3.1 We do not collect, request or receive any of the following:

  • (a) your name, email address, telephone number, postal address or any other contact details;
  • (b) account credentials of any kind, the App having no registration or sign-in function;
  • (c) device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA, AAID) or other persistent identifiers;
  • (d) usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports or performance measurements;
  • (e) precise or approximate location data;
  • (f) contacts, photographs, calendars, files or health data;
  • (g) payment or financial information, the App containing no purchases or subscriptions;
  • (h) cookies or comparable web-tracking technologies.

3.2 The Android edition of the App is published without the INTERNET permission, and the iOS edition contains no networking code. The App is therefore technically incapable of transmitting information to us or to any third party.

4. Information stored on your device

4.1 In order to provide spaced repetition, the App records and stores the following in local storage on your device:

  • (a) learning progress — the exercises, rules, verbs, words and texts you have seen, together with their scheduling data;
  • (b) answer history — correct and incorrect answers, including the incorrect options selected, so that a mistake can be explained and repeated later;
  • (c) activity record — a local log of your practice sessions;
  • (d) preferences — selected language, playback speed, chosen voices, reminder time, and any item you have marked as already known.

4.2 This information is written to the private storage area assigned to the App by the operating system. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no technical means of accessing, retrieving or requesting it.

4.3 If device backups are enabled on your device (iCloud Backup or the Google backup service), App data may be included in such backups. Those backups are created, stored and controlled by Apple or Google in accordance with their own privacy policies and your device settings, and not by us.

5. Device permissions

5.1 Microphone and speech recognition

5.1.1 The "Say it" exercise requires access to the microphone in order to evaluate the phrase you pronounce.

5.1.2 Permission is requested only when that exercise is first used, is entirely optional, and may be declined or subsequently revoked in your device settings. All other functions of the App remain available if permission is withheld.

5.1.3 Where the exercise is used, the captured audio is passed to the speech-recognition service built into your operating system — the Speech framework on iOS, the platform speech recognizer on Android. On Android, the App requests offline recognition where the device supports it.

5.1.4 Depending on your device, the language selected and your system settings, the operating system may process that audio on servers operated by Apple Inc. or Google LLC, in accordance with their respective privacy policies. Such processing is carried out by Apple or Google as independent controllers and is outside our control. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5.1.5 The App does not record audio to a file, does not retain audio, and does not transmit audio to us or to any other party of its own accord. The recognised text is compared with the expected phrase, used to score the exercise, and then discarded.

5.2 Notifications

5.2.1 If you enable a daily reminder, it is scheduled locally by your device at the time you select.

5.2.2 No push-notification server is used and no information is transmitted in connection with reminders.

5.2.3 Reminders may be disabled at any time within the App or in your device settings.

6. Purposes of processing

6.1 The only processing that takes place occurs locally on your device, for the purposes of presenting exercises, scheduling repetition, and displaying your own progress.

6.2 No information is used for profiling, behavioural advertising, or automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects.

6.3 The App includes a function by which you may generate a summary of your progress in Markdown format and share it using your device's standard sharing interface. Nothing is exported unless you initiate it, and any service to which you send such a file is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

7. Third-party services

7.1 The App incorporates no third-party software development kits for analytics, advertising, attribution or crash reporting.

7.2 The sole third-party processing that may arise is the operating-system speech recognition described in section 5.1, provided by Apple Inc. or Google LLC.

7.3 The application stores through which the App is distributed may collect information in connection with the download and installation of the App. Such collection is governed by the privacy policies of Apple Inc. and Google LLC respectively, and is not within our control.

8. Disclosure of information

8.1 We do not sell, rent, trade, disclose or otherwise share personal information, as we do not collect any.

8.2 As no information reaches us, we hold nothing capable of being disclosed to any third party, including in response to a legal or governmental request.

9. Retention and deletion

9.1 We retain no information, as we receive none.

9.2 Information stored locally is retained on your device until you remove it. You may do so at any time:

  • (a) by selecting Settings → "Start over from zero", which erases the stored progress for the language then selected;
  • (b) by uninstalling the App, which removes all of its stored data from the device.

9.3 Data contained in a device backup is removed in accordance with the backup settings and retention rules of Apple or Google.

10. Security

10.1 App data remains within the sandboxed storage area provided by the operating system and is protected by the security model of that operating system, including device-level encryption where enabled.

10.2 Because no information is transmitted, there is no transmission capable of interception, and no server on which your data could be exposed.

10.3 Responsibility for the physical and logical security of the device itself — including passcode, biometric lock and device encryption — rests with you.

11. International transfers

11.1 We carry out no international transfer of personal data, as no data leaves your device by any act of ours.

11.2 Any transfer arising from operating-system speech recognition or from a device backup is effected by Apple or Google under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

12. Children's privacy

12.1 The App is suitable for users of all ages and contains no user-generated content, messaging or social features.

12.2 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, or under the equivalent minimum age in the user's jurisdiction, for the reason that we collect no personal information from any user.

12.3 As no collection takes place, no verifiable parental consent mechanism is required under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or Article 8 of the GDPR.

13. Your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR

13.1 Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies to you, you hold rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

13.2 Because we neither collect nor hold any personal data relating to you, there is no data in our possession to which a request for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability could relate. The controls described in section 9.2 afford you complete and immediate control over all information the App stores.

13.3 Your right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement is unaffected by this Policy.

14. Information for California residents

14.1 We do not collect any of the categories of personal information enumerated in Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140, and we collect no sensitive personal information.

14.2 We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months and do not do so.

14.3 We offer no financial incentive in exchange for personal information, and no user is subject to discriminatory treatment for exercising any right under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA.

15.3 Those declarations are consistent with this Policy and with the technical characteristics of the App described in section 3.2.
