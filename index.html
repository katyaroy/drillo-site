What is inside

A verb engine, not a list

Around 450 verbs per language, conjugated across every tense — including the 129 Dutch separable verbs, whose prefix splits in a main clause and sits inside the participle.

Grammar from A1 to C1

Each rule is written to be read once, then drilled immediately. Nine kinds of drill, from fill-in to listening to spelling-to-sound.

Stories worth finishing

Eight serialised series of six episodes per language — everyday life, fashion, health, food, relationships, love, wine and news — each with its own cast.

Words that carry their article

Vocabulary is never taught bare: Dutch nouns always arrive with de or het, and gender is drilled as part of the word.

Sound & spelling

How a written word is actually said, taught against words you can already pronounce rather than against phonetic notation.

Commute mode

Hands-free listening you can run on a bike or a train with the screen locked. Tap a word in any text to see what it means.
