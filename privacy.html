Drillo

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

The short version

Drillo collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your learning progress never leaves your phone, and the app makes no network connections to us or to anyone else.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Drillo mobile app for iPhone, iPad and Android ("Drillo", "the app"). It explains what the app does with information on your device. Because Drillo has no servers, there is no other place where your information could be held.

Information the app stores on your device

Drillo keeps a record of your learning so that spaced repetition can work. All of it is written to your device's own private app storage and stays there:

  • Which cards, rules, verbs, words and texts you have seen, and when they are next due
  • Your answers — right and wrong — and the wrong options you picked, so the app can explain a mistake and bring it back
  • A local activity log of your sessions
  • Your settings: chosen language, speaking speed, voices, reminder time, and which items you have marked "I know this"

This information is never transmitted to us. We cannot see it, and we have no way to request it. If your device is set to back itself up (iCloud Backup or Google's backup service), this app data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy and controlled by your device settings — not by Drillo.

Microphone and speech recognition

The "Say it" drill listens while you say a phrase so the app can check what you said. This is entirely optional: permission is only requested when you first use that drill, you can decline it, and every other part of Drillo works without it.

When you use it, the audio is handed to your device's own speech-recognition service — Apple's Speech framework on iOS, Android's speech recognizer on Android — which converts it to text and returns the text to the app. On Android, Drillo asks for offline recognition where the device supports it. Depending on your device, language and settings, the operating system may process that audio on Apple's or Google's servers, under Apple's or Google's own privacy policy.

Drillo itself never records to a file, never stores audio and never sends audio anywhere of its own accord. The recognised text is compared with the expected phrase, used to mark the card, and then discarded.

Notifications

If you turn on a daily reminder, it is scheduled locally by your device at the time you choose. There is no push server and no message is sent over the internet. You can turn reminders off inside the app or in your system settings at any time.

Exporting your progress

Drillo can generate a Markdown summary of your progress for you to copy or share — for example, to paste into an AI assistant to keep studying. Nothing is exported unless you tap the button, and where it goes afterwards is entirely your choice, made through your device's standard share sheet. Once you send that file somewhere, the privacy policy of whatever service you send it to applies.

What Drillo does not do

  • No account, login, email address or profile — the app never asks who you are
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No location, contacts, photos, calendar or health data
  • No cookies or web tracking
  • No selling, sharing or renting of personal information, because none is collected

Drillo's App Store privacy label and Google Play Data safety section are declared as no data collected, which matches what the app does.

Children

Drillo is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), because we do not collect data from any user.

Your control over your data

Everything Drillo stores is on your device and under your control:

  • Reset: Settings → "Start over from zero" erases the progress for that language
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all of its stored data from your device
  • Permissions: microphone and notification access can be revoked at any time in your system settings

Rights such as access, correction, portability and erasure under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete on your behalf — the controls above are the complete picture.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
